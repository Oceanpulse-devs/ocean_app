🛒 1. Standard Buy & Sell Marketplace
•	What it is: Users can post items they want to sell and browse items they want to buy.
•	How it works: Just like any e-commerce platform, items are listed by sellers with prices, images, and descriptions. Buyers can message sellers and purchase directly.
________________________________________
👥 2. Group Buy
•	What it is: Multiple buyers team up to purchase an item together in bulk.
•	How it works:
o	Users search for an item.
o	If no existing Group Buy exists, they are prompted to either cancel or "Post to Buyer Needs Board".
o	This board is a special place where users describe what they want to buy and sellers respond.
o	Once enough buyers commit, the seller delivers at a discounted group rate.
________________________________________
🔄 3. Swap Deals
•	What it is: A trade system where users can exchange items directly without cash.
•	How it works:
o	Sellers can list items with “Swap Only” or “Swap & Sell.”
o	Interested users send swap offers with their own listed items.
o	Both parties must approve before a deal is finalized.
________________________________________
📊 4. Market Survey
•	What it is: A tool for sellers/buyers to know the prices of various and items/products 
•	How it works: Ocean collects user search data and shares anonymous insights on what buyers are actively looking for. (for sellers)
________________________________________
🧠 5. Buyer Search Insight
•	What it is: Shows buyers what other people are searching for but can't find.
•	How it works: Helps buyers join a demand trend or adjust their expectations based on what’s scarce or trending.
________________________________________
📮 6. Post to Buyer Needs Board
•	What it is: A special board where buyers post exactly what they want to buy.
•	How it works:
o	If someone searches for an item and finds nothing, they’re offered a “Post to Buyer Needs” option.
o	Sellers subscribed to special tiers will see this list and can meet that demand.
📦 8. Product AR View (Augmented Reality)
•	What it is: Buyers can view 3D product models using their phone camera before buying.
•	How it works:
o	Sellers record a guided video following Ocean’s AR instructions.
o	Ocean converts this into a 3D model.
o	Buyers can place the item in their space virtually before buying.
9. Global Market Access
•	What it is: Ocean is not limited to Nigeria; it is designed to scale into American, Asian, and global markets.
•	
🔔 10. Buyer Notifications
•	What it is: Real-time alerts for saved searches, price drops, group buy updates.
•	How it works: Users get push notifications when:
o	New group buys for their item are created.
o	A swap match is found.
o	Sellers respond to their Buyer Needs posts.
🧾 11. Verified Seller Badges
•	What it is: Trust and credibility system for power sellers.
•	How it works:
o	Verified sellers go through ID, payment, and listing checks.
o	They get more visibility, better placement, and access to premium features.
💬 12. In-App Chat for Negotiations
•	What it is: Secure direct messaging between buyers and sellers.
•	How it works:
o	Text/chat support with automated message templates.
o	Group chat option for group buys.
13. Analytics Dashboard (for Sellers)
•	What it is: Sellers can view metrics like:
o	Views per listing
o	Search trends
o	AR view stats
🔔 16. Seller Notifications
What it is:
Real-time alerts that help sellers respond faster to opportunities, buyer actions, and market trends.
How it works:
Sellers receive in-app or push notifications for the following events:
🔍 Trigger	📲 Notification Message Example
A buyer posts on the Buyer Needs Board	“New buyer is looking for ‘air fryer’ in Lagos. Tap to respond.”
A Group Buy request is posted in your category	“Group Buy created for ‘Bluetooth Speakers.’ Add your deal?”
Someone adds your product to cart or watchlist	“10 users are watching your ‘PlayStation 5.’ Consider offering a discount?”
You’ve been sent a swap offer	“A user offered their ‘iPhone 11’ for your ‘Samsung S21’.”
Your product gets AR views	“Your ‘Office Chair’ was viewed in AR by 5 users today.”
A search insight matches your listings	“People in Abuja searched for ‘3-seater couch.’ Do you want to view their profile and talk to them
A subscription renewal or expiry reminder	“Your Tide plan expires in 3 days. Renew now to keep seeing Buyer Needs.”

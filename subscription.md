🏷️ Seller Features
•	Free Listings (limited for free plan) – Post items for sale.
•	Unlimited Listings – For paid tiers.
•	Group Buy Creation – Create group deals that buyers can join.
•	Product Demand – Dashboard showing what products are trending across the app.
•	Buyer Needs Board Access – See posts from buyers requesting products.
•	Buyer Search Insights – See what buyers are searching for but not finding (paid tier).
•	Swap Deals – Offer exchanges instead of cash.
•	Auction Listings – List items for bidding.
•	Shopfront Customization – Add logo, banners, and shop bio.
•	Promotional Boosts – Push products to the top of search feeds.
•	Verified Seller Badge – Builds trust with buyers.
•	Advanced Analytics (subscription): sales insights, 
•	Notifications – Alerts when a buyer bids, joins a group buy, posts a need, or searches for a seller’s item.

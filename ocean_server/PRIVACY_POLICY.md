# 🔐 BLUEWHALE — PRIVACY POLICY
*Last updated: August 8, 2026*
*Target URL: https://bluewhalemarketplace.com/privacy-policy*

---

## 1. Information We Collect
- Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number
- Account credentials & authentication tokens
- Location data & store addresses
- Device identifiers & operating system metrics
- Payment transaction metadata (handled securely via third-party processors)
- In-app messages, search requests, and product listings
- Platform usage & activity logs

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
To operate the marketplace, process transactions, deliver buyer request notifications, improve features, personalize content, prevent fraud, and support security analytics.

---

## 3. Sharing of Information
We may share necessary data with payment processors (Flutterwave, Apple Pay, Google Pay), analytics providers, legal authorities (when mandated by law), and aggregated analytics partners.

---

## 4. Data Security
We use industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliant gateways to protect your personal data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Cookies & Tracking
We use essential cookies and session tokens to maintain user logins, performance metrics, and security preferences.

---

## 6. Your Rights
You may request access to your personal data, request corrections, opt out of marketing communications, or delete your account data.

---

## 7. Data Retention
We retain account and transaction data only as long as necessary for active business operations or legal compliance.

---

## 8. International Users
Your data may be stored and processed on servers located in secure global data center regions.

---

## 9. Children Privacy
Bluewhale Marketplace is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and older.

---

## 10. Policy Changes
We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Notice will be provided via the platform.

---

## 11. Account & Data Deletion Requests
You can request full account deletion inside the app by navigating to **Settings → Delete Account**. Alternatively, you can submit an online request at [https://bluewhalemarketplace.com/delete-account](https://bluewhalemarketplace.com/delete-account).

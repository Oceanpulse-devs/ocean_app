Privacy Policy

Section 01

1. Information Bluewhale Collects

Bluewhale Marketplace collects necessary personal information and usage data to provide, operate, and secure our services:

  • Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number

  • Device identifiers & operating system metrics

2. How Bluewhale Uses Your Information

Bluewhale uses your information to operate the marketplace, process transactions, deliver buyer request notifications, improve platform features, personalize content, prevent fraud, and enforce platform security analytics.

Section 03

Bluewhale uses industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliant gateways to protect your personal data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

Section 05

5. Cookies & Tracking

Bluewhale uses essential cookies, local storage, and session tokens to maintain user logins, performance metrics, and security preferences.

Section 06

6. Your Rights

You may request access to your personal data, request corrections, opt out of marketing communications, or delete your Bluewhale account data at any time.

Section 07

7. Data Retention

Bluewhale retains account and transaction data only as long as necessary for active business operations or legal compliance.

Section 08

8. International Users

Your data may be stored and processed by Bluewhale on servers located in secure global data center regions.

Section 09

9. Children's Privacy

Bluewhale Marketplace is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and older. Bluewhale does not knowingly collect data from minors.

Section 10

10. Policy Changes

Bluewhale reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Notice of significant changes will be provided via the platform.

Alternatively, you can submit an online account deletion request directly at https://bluewhalemarketplace.com/delete-account.
